01 — About

I'm a physics researcher working at the intersection of scientific computing and deep learning. My research centers on operator learning (Fourier Neural Operators / PINO) and physics-informed neural networks for forward and inverse PDE problems, most recently at IISc Bangalore.

A thread running through these projects is a single question — what has a model actually learned, and what is it still missing? It's what led me to build the Residual Learnability Score, and it's why I'm drawn to interpretability: understanding not just how well a model performs, but what it has and hasn't captured.
